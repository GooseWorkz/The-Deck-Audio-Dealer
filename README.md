Ideas/Thoughs/READ ME

I am designing an interactive page, depicting a deck of cards to the right and when you click on the deck, a card is delt to the left. When a card has been delt, it has left the stack and cannot be delt again. Once the card you want is in the "delt" pile, you can click on said card and it will come to life and give you more information on the portrait. Upon clicking the card a second time, if should tell you where the person is from. To reload, one must reload the whole page.

This animation is built off of the podcast, The Deck on Spotify. Every card is the depiction of a missing persons and their corresponing date and state from which they are missing. Depending upon the card shown, the Audio player will change to a different episode.

The animations are nothing to crazy, just some flipping and writing with keyframes to slow or speed them up.
